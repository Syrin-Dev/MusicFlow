
URGENT FIX REQUIRED: Google "redirect_uri_mismatch" Error

The screenshot you uploaded confirms the error code: 400: redirect_uri_mismatch.

This means your Vercel app is telling Google to send the user back to:
https://hievly.com/api/auth/callback/google

BUT, your Google Cloud Console does NOT have this exact URL in the "Authorized redirect URIs" list.

You MUST do this to fix it:

1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials
2. Select your project.
3. Click on the "Hievly Web" (or similar) OAuth 2.0 Client ID.
4. Scroll down to "Authorized redirect URIs".
5. Paste this EXACT link:
   https://hievly.com/api/auth/callback/google
6. Click SAVE.

Wait 5 minutes, then try again.
