## 2024-05-18 - [ARIA Labels & Focus States for PlayerBar]
**Learning:** Many interactive icon-only buttons in custom media players lack basic `aria-label`, `title`, and keyboard focus styling.
**Action:** Consistently add `aria-label`, conditional `title` tooltips, and `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-white` styling to custom control buttons for full keyboard and screen reader accessibility.
