## 2026-04-08 - Adding Accessible Seek Scrubber\n**Learning:** Implementing custom seek bars and custom expansion buttons out of `div`s without correct ARIA labels, roles or keybindings is detrimental for screen readers and keyboard users. Providing a keyboard-navigable seeker mapping arrow keys to `currentTime` adjustments improves a11y drastically.\n**Action:** Add `role="slider"`, `tabIndex={0}`, `aria-valuemin` / max / now / text, and handle `onKeyDown` with arrow keys when custom scrubbers are encountered.
